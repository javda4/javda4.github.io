Your Privacy

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 27, 2026

The short version

  • We only collect what you choose to send us through our Contact Us form: your name, email address and message.
  • We use it only to reply to you. We never sell it, and we don't share it with partners or sponsors.
  • To prevent spam, we keep a scrambled, unreadable fingerprint of your IP address for no more than 24 hours.
  • Our website doesn't use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers.
  • You can ask us to delete your information at any time.

Who we are

Just For You Foundation ("we", "us") is a 501(c)(3) nonprofit organization in Pembroke Pines, Florida, that supports children battling cancer and their families. This policy explains how we handle information collected through our website. If you have any questions, please contact us.

Information we collect

When you contact us

When you send a message through our Contact Us form, we collect:

  • Your name and email address, so we can reply.
  • Your message, which is whatever you choose to tell us.
  • The date and time you sent it.
  • A scrambled fingerprint of your IP address (your device's network address). We don't store the address itself. It's run through a one-way, secret-keyed function (a salted HMAC-SHA256 hash), so it can't be read or turned back into your IP address. We use it only to stop anyone from flooding the form with spam, by limiting how many messages can be sent from one place in a short time. It's automatically deleted within 24 hours, while your name, email and message are kept so we can respond.

Sensitive information you choose to share

Many families who write to us are going through a child's illness, and you may choose to tell us about a diagnosis, treatment or financial hardship. We treat anything you share with care. It's used only to understand how we can help and to respond to you, and only our team can see it. Please share only what you're comfortable with. You never need to send medical records or detailed health information through the website.

Information we don't collect

  • Our website doesn't set cookies or use analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • There are no user accounts or passwords.
  • The donation and newsletter forms on our site aren't connected yet and don't collect or store anything. If we turn them on, we'll update this policy first.

Information our service providers see automatically

Like any website, loading our pages means your browser connects to the services that deliver them. These services may record your IP address, browser type and the page requested in their own server logs, under their own privacy policies. They're listed in the table below.

How we use your information

  • To read and respond to your message.
  • To provide the support, partnership or volunteer information you asked about.
  • To keep the contact form safe from spam and abuse.

We don't sell, rent or trade your personal information, we don't use it for advertising, and we won't add you to a mailing list without your permission.

How we protect your information

  • Messages are stored in a private database that isn't reachable from the public website. Only authorized members of our team can read them.
  • All connections to our website and database are encrypted (HTTPS).
  • The form checks every submission and rejects code or markup, which helps protect against malicious input.
  • Rate limiting and automated spam checks protect the form from abuse.
  • Access keys are stored encrypted and are never placed on the public website.

No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect what you share with us.

How long we keep it

  • IP fingerprint: automatically deleted within 24 hours.
  • Name, email and message: kept for as long as we need them to respond to you and to keep a record of our conversation. You can ask us to delete them at any time.

Your choices and rights

You can ask us to:

  • tell you what information we hold about you,
  • correct it, or
  • delete it.

Just email info@justforyoufoundation.org from the address you used to contact us, and we'll respond as soon as we can. Depending on where you live, you may have additional rights under local law, and we'll honor them.

Children's privacy

Our website is meant for parents, caregivers, supporters and partners. It isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has sent us information, please contact us and we'll delete it.

Links to other websites

Our site links to partner organizations and social media pages. Those sites have their own privacy practices, and we're not responsible for them.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy as our website changes, for example if we begin accepting donations online. When we do, we'll post the new version here and update the effective date above.
