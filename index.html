Who We Are

We Show Up When It Matters Most

A hospital room can feel like the loneliest place in the world. Just For You Foundation exists to change that, supporting children battling cancer, and the families holding everything together around them, with real, tangible help from diagnosis through recovery.

Comfort

Hospital care packages, comfort items, and small moments of joy delivered directly to a child's bedside.

Financial Relief

Help with medical bills, meals, and everyday costs so families can focus on their child instead of their finances.

Community

A community that shows up when it matters most, walking beside families through diagnosis, treatment, and recovery.

A Message From Jason

Why I Started This Foundation

I started Just For You Foundation after watching a child I love fight through a diagnosis no family should ever have to hear. I saw how cancer doesn't just affect the child, it reshapes an entire family overnight. Bills pile up. Routines disappear. Fear moves in.

I also saw how much a single act of kindness could mean in the middle of that fight, a paid bill, a warm meal, or someone simply showing up. This foundation is a way of making sure no family faces that fight without support standing beside them.

Every program we run and every dollar we raise is for the kids who are still fighting, and the families who refuse to give up on them.

— Jason

Our Dream

Named For A Woman Who Always Showed Up

This foundation carries the legacy of Mama Luca, Jason's grandmother, born in St. Marc and raised in Port-au-Prince, Haiti, who raised fifteen children with her husband Luc over more than fifty-five years of marriage.

Mama Luca lived by one simple rule: when someone was in need, you showed up. Her door was always open, and she fed, clothed, encouraged, and cared for everyone around her without hesitation, no matter how little she had. She taught that compassion is more than something you feel, it is something you put into action.

When a child is facing a life-altering illness, the entire family carries that journey. Founded by Jason Derulo and co-founded by his mother, Joyce, Just For You Foundation exists to surround those families with the same unconditional love and care Mama Luca gave so freely throughout her life.

Her love became our legacy. Our dream is to pass that love forward.
